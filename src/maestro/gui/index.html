Maestro: violin feedback

Upload a video (or audio) of yourself playing and the score you played from.

Lay the page flat in good light and hold the camera square above it, filling the view; take one photo per page.

Written feedback

Every report writes feedback for you automatically and lets you ask follow-up questions. It is written by a small model that runs on this computer through Ollama (free, private, works offline; the models below are small enough for any recent computer or iPhone). Install Ollama and start it, then download a model once. Only a short summary of numbers (bar numbers, errors in cents and milliseconds, scores) and your chat questions reach the model, never the recording or score.
