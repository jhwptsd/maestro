Maestro: violin feedback

Upload a video (or audio) of yourself playing and the score you played from.

Written feedback from an AI (optional)

After an analysis you can ask Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini to turn the results into feedback. Connect one with your own API key (a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini app subscription cannot be linked: the providers only offer keys for developers, billed by use). Get a key: Claude, OpenAI, Google AI Studio. The key is kept on this computer only. What is sent to the provider is a short summary of numbers (bar numbers, errors in cents and milliseconds, scores): never your recording, video or score; you can read it on the report page before asking.
