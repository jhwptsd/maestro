Written feedback from an AI

Once connected, every report writes feedback for you automatically and lets you ask the AI follow-up questions. Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or another service with your own API key (a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini app subscription cannot be linked: the providers only offer keys for developers, billed by use; Gemini and Groq have free tiers). Get a key: Claude, OpenAI, Google AI Studio, Groq, OpenRouter. Or skip the key: sign in to OpenRouter, which has free models. The key is kept on this computer only. What is sent to the provider is a short summary of numbers (bar numbers, errors in cents and milliseconds, scores) and what you type in the chat: never your recording, video or score; the report page shows the summary.
